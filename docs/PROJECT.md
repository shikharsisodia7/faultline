# About this project

Faultline is an open-source project: a flight recorder and time machine for software failures, built on DeepSpace and published under the MIT license.

## What is real

- The replay, correlation, and simulation engines are real, deterministic, and covered by unit tests.
- Every number in the demo comes from running the simulator. Nothing is hard-coded to look good; decisions that make an outage worse are shown as worse.
- The demo incidents are **synthetic**: they were generated by the simulator so that their logs, alerts, and "what if" baselines agree.

## What is not claimed

- No customers, funding, testimonials, or usage numbers. There are none.
- Correlation scores are heuristic, not probabilities, and have not been calibrated against labeled incidents.
- Counterfactual results are model estimates under stated assumptions, not predictions about a real system.
